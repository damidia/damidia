# 🗺 RoMap — Map-Based Field Sales CRM (Capstone)

### 🚀 Live MVP
https://romap-field-pro.base44.app/

RoMap is a **mobile-first, map-centric CRM** designed specifically for **B2B field sales teams**.

Unlike traditional CRMs built around spreadsheets and lists, RoMap treats geography as the core workflow layer.

Sales reps can:
- Log visits directly on a map
- Manage territories visually
- Track tasks and follow-ups by location
- Coordinate activities across a shared account

---

## 🚩 Problem

Traditional CRMs are often:
- Expensive for small sales teams
- Complex to navigate
- Optimized for desk workflows rather than field work

Field sales teams instead:
- Operate geographically
- Move between physical businesses
- Need fast mobile-first workflows

RoMap was designed to reduce friction for real-world field sales activity.

---

## 📈 Product Thinking

Key product questions explored:
- How can reps log activity in under 10 seconds?
- What workflows occur most frequently in the field?
- What information should managers see versus reps?
- How can map interactions feel intuitive on mobile devices?
- How do we reduce friction compared to traditional CRMs?

---

## 🎯 Product Goals

Build an MVP supporting:
- Multi-user organizations
- Role-based permissions
- Shared and isolated data
- Map-based CRM workflows
- End-to-end business management functionality
